## 📈 Market Landscape & Overview 🌍

> **Market Size & Structure:** The global omnichannel retail commerce software market is estimated at **$11.8 Billion in 2026** and is projected to reach **$24.5 Billion by 2032** (CAGR ~12.9%). The market is **moderately fragmented**, featuring high-valuation mega-vendors (Oracle, Salesforce, SAP) alongside specialized composable MACH engines (commercetools, VTEX) and rapid-growth headless SaaS ecosystems (Shopify Enterprise). 📊

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects 📦

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Odoo eCommerce](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/odoo/odoo?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/stargazers)  
  **Open-source business suite with integrated e-commerce**, LGPL-3.0 licensed. AI website builder, drag-and-drop product pages, real-time stock management, click-and-collect, and integrated POS. Free forever with domain name included. 🏢

- **[Medusa](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/medusajs/medusa?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/stargazers)  
  **The world’s most flexible commerce platform**, MIT licensed. Modular architecture with 22+ commerce modules (Cart, Order, Payment, Fulfillment, Inventory). Workflows engine for multi-step operations with rollback. TypeScript/Node.js + PostgreSQL. Self-hostable or Medusa Cloud. 🐙

- **[Bagisto](https://github.com/bagisto/bagisto)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bagisto/bagisto?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bagisto/bagisto/stargazers)  
  **Free and open-source Laravel e-commerce framework**, MIT licensed. Built for omnichannel retail with integrated POS system, multi-warehouse inventory management, GraphQL API, and multi-tenant marketplace capabilities. 🛍️

- **[Saleor](https://github.com/saleor/saleor)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/saleor/saleor?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/saleor/saleor/stargazers)  
  **Leading open-source MACH commerce platform**, BSD-3-Clause licensed. GraphQL-first API, AI-ready storefront foundation, 45+ dashboard extension mount points. Channels for multi-surface commerce (web, mobile, POS). Saleor Cloud available. ⛵

- **[Spree Commerce](https://github.com/spree/spree)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/spree/spree?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/spree/spree/stargazers)  
  **Open-source omnichannel commerce for DTC, B2B, and marketplaces**, BSD-3-Clause licensed. REST APIs, TypeScript SDKs, Next.js storefront. Seller onboarding with commission engine, B2B price lists, multi-tenant SaaS Enterprise Edition. Zero platform fees. 🛒

- **[Reaction Commerce](https://github.com/reactioncommerce/reaction)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/reactioncommerce/reaction?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/reactioncommerce/reaction/stargazers)  
  **Real-time GraphQL headless commerce engine**, GPL-3.0 licensed. Event-driven Node.js & Microservices architecture designed for modern omnichannel digital retail and rapid headless custom storefront integration. ⚛️

- **[Magento Open Source](https://github.com/magento/magento2)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/magento/magento2?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/magento/magento2/stargazers)  
  **The industry-standard PHP e-commerce platform**, OSL-3.0 licensed. Extensive ecosystem with thousands of omnichannel POS plugins, B2B suite integrations, multi-store architecture, and robust inventory control. 🟠

- **[WooCommerce](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/woocommerce/woocommerce?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce/stargazers)  
  **WordPress-based open-source e-commerce platform**, GPL-3.0 licensed. Powers over 40% of all online stores worldwide. POS extensions (WCPOS, OpenPOS) enable seamless omnichannel retail with real-time stock sync and barcode scanning. 🌐

- **[nopCommerce](https://github.com/nopSolutions/nopCommerce)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nopSolutions/nopCommerce?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/nopSolutions/nopCommerce/stargazers)  
  **The most popular ASP.NET Core e-commerce platform**, MIT licensed. 250,000+ community members. Features AI content tools, B2B workflows, multi-store & multi-vendor retail capabilities. Free and open-source since 2008. 💻

- **[PrestaShop](https://github.com/prestashop/prestashop)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/prestashop/prestashop?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/prestashop/prestashop/stargazers)  
  **Global open-source e-commerce solution**, OSL-3.0 licensed. Highly customizable Symfony-based core with extensive module marketplace for POS synchronization, multi-store management, and international omnichannel selling. 🐧

- **[Sylius](https://github.com/Sylius/Sylius)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Sylius/Sylius?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Sylius/Sylius/stargazers)  
  **Headless e-commerce framework for Symfony**, MIT licensed. Full control over data model, business logic, and checkout flow. Used by Carrefour, Forbes, PwC, and Mytheresa. Sylius Plus adds RBAC, multi-source inventory, and B2B pricing engine. 🚀

- **[Vendure](https://github.com/vendure-ecommerce/vendure)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/vendure-ecommerce/vendure?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/vendure-ecommerce/vendure/stargazers)  
  **TypeScript-first commerce framework for developers**, MIT licensed. NestJS + GraphQL + PostgreSQL/MySQL. Plugin architecture with Strategy pattern for behavior replacement. 40,000+ monthly npm downloads. Used by IBM and Swile. 🛠️

- **[Shopware](https://github.com/shopware/shopware)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/shopware/shopware?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/shopware/shopware/stargazers)  
  **Open-source headless commerce platform**, MIT licensed. Built on Symfony and Vue.js. Offers Rule Builder, Flow Builder, and omnichannel capabilities designed for enterprise B2C and B2B operations. 🎨

- **[ShopCube](https://github.com/Abdur-rahmaanJ/shopcube)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Abdur-rahmaanJ/shopcube?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Abdur-rahmaanJ/shopcube/stargazers)  
  **E-commerce and POS for Python shops**, MIT licensed. Built on Shopyo Flask framework. Inventory with multi-location, purchase orders, POS with shift management, customer groups, and role-based access. 🐍

- **[Spwig Commerce](https://github.com/Spwig/commerce)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Spwig/commerce?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Spwig/commerce/stargazers)  
  **Runtime feature-gated commerce platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. Community/Pro/Enterprise run same binary with license flags. Provider SDKs (Apache-2.0) enable proprietary payment/shipping integrations without AGPL contamination. 🔑

## 🛠️ How to Contribute 🤝

Contributions are welcome! Follow these steps to submit new omnichannel retail platforms or open-source commerce software:

1. 🍴 **Fork** the repository.
2. 📝 **Add/edit** entries in `README.md` maintaining table/list structure and formatting.
3. 🔗 Include project title, official website/GitHub link, exact Stars_Count, license, and brief description.
4. 🚀 Submit a **Pull Request** with a descriptive summary of your changes.
